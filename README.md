# 🐍 World Sync: Jormungandr

A [Ragnarok Offline](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app) mod that makes the
**Jormungandr** saga playable on the maps World Sync brought to the server. This first
release is **Episode 20, The Undying One**, start to finish.

## ❄️ What's in it

- 🏔️ **The Ancient Ice Canyon**: Icewinds, mana absorption devices, and Iwin soldiers who aren't Iwins.
- 🚪 **The Lair of the Rgans**: the door at the back of the Snake God's Nest finally opens.
- 🌿 **The Sacred Roots**: dig snakeberries, help five very strange Rgans, earn Sarekgand's trust.
- 🌀 **The Serpent's Maze**: Bagot's gate, Rasgand himself, and a white cat.
- 🍓 **A guest in the Ice Castle**: His Holiness Nadyagand demands snacks.
- 🧊 **The Frozen Zone** and the **Nest of Small Branches**, with Kororo, the Diving Iwin and Nyar.
- ⚔️ **Separated Sanctuary** memorial dungeon: break the undying bishop.

Full dialogue, the client's own quest log (83 quests), the official steps, kill goals and
rewards, with cut-in portraits. 119 of the official NPCs that World Sync Extras placed as
placeholders on these maps now play their part.

## 🗺️ How to start

Finish Issgard's Episode 19, reach **level 200**, then talk to **Horuru** in the adventurers'
lodge of the Ice Castle (`icas_in`). You'll need **Rgan Transformation Scrolls** (Lunch, on the
Frozen Scale Glacier, makes them); the Rgans of the Sacred Roots only talk to Rgans.

No Episode 19 yet? Tick **Start Episode 20 without finishing Episode 19** under
Settings → Mods.

## 📦 Requirements

- Ragnarok Offline **1.6.7** or later, renewal: the World Sync maps (`jor_*`, `icas_in2`, `1@twig`).
- A client with the Episode 20 data (recent iRO or kRO).
- Recommended: World Sync Extras 1.3.0 or later, which leaves these NPCs to this mod.

## 📝 Notes

- No official script exists for this episode. Everything is written from the kRO client's
  quest text and navigation tables (English by [ROenglishRE](https://github.com/llchrisll/ROenglishRE));
  rewards and the boss's stats follow Episode 19 and the episode's other boss where the
  official numbers aren't published.
- Character variables are `wsj_ep20`, `wsj_ep20b` and `wsj_ep20c`.
- Coming next: the episode's dailies and exchangers, then Episode 21 (Lunaforma, Alberta of old).
