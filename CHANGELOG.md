# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Episode 20, The Undying One, from start to finish (83 quests in the quest log):
  - The Ancient Ice Canyon: Icewinds, mana absorption devices and Iwin soldiers who aren't Iwins.
  - The Lair of the Rgans: the door of the Snake God's Nest opens, the Roots of the Serpent God, a borrowed bishop.
  - The Sacred Roots: snakeberries, five Rgans who need help, and Sarekgand.
  - The Serpent's Maze, a gate built by Bagot, Rasgand himself, and a white cat.
  - A guest in the Ice Castle: His Holiness Nadyagand.
  - The Frozen Zone and the Nest of Small Branches, with Kororo, the Diving Iwin and Nyar.
  - The memorial dungeon Separated Sanctuary (1@twig): break the undying Rasgand.
- 119 of World Sync Extras' placeholder NPCs on these maps now have their part in the story.
- Setting `skip_ep19`: start the episode without Episode 19.
